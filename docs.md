Here's how to use the petstore yaml:

# Look at the spec
Open the file in your personal IDE like VS Code.

# Use the endpoints
1. Setup Postman account
2. Import the specification
3. Start making requests
4. Troubleshoot errors and if needed contact support at ben.yrps+support@gmail.com


# Make Requests and order from the Pet Store
1. Make requests, process the response from the pet store.
2. Wait for your order to arrive
3. Give the items to your pets.

- Feed your fish
- Give your cat a toy
- Walk your dog on a new leash
